# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the problem or force that requires a decision? What constraints apply
(compatibility, performance, the multi-TFM target set, the async-only rule,
the SDK surface, downstream consumers)? State the facts, not the decision.

## Decision

The choice that was made, in one or two sentences, stated as an active
resolution ("We compile property getters with expression trees...").

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include the
downsides accepted, the alternatives rejected and why, and any follow-up
constraint the decision imposes on future work.
